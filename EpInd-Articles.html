Epigraphia Indica is the Archaeological Survey of India's journal of epigraphy, issued in 42 volumes from 1888. Each article edits one or more inscriptions, most of them copper-plate grants and stone records: an introduction (the find-spot, the object, script and language, the dynasty and the date, the places named), then the text line by line, a translation, footnotes and the plates. Many of the inscriptions in South Indian Inscriptions and the Annual Report on Epigraphy were first published in full here.

Please verify before you quote. These articles were read from the scanned volumes by machine (OCR) and put back together page by page. Mistakes may remain, most of all in Devanagari and other Indic script, and in the small footnote numbers. Please check the printed volume before using the text.
