THE AGE OF PATTUPPĀTTU

BY

Mr. T. A. RAMALINGAM CHETTIAR B.A., B.L.

PATTUPPATTU or the “Ten Idylls” is, as the name denotes, a collection of ten exquisite Tamil Poems. They are among the best in the Tamil Literature and can bear comparison with any ten poems in any language. Of their high literary merit, the keen insight shown by their authors into human feelings, and the very fine portrait of incidents in human life and natural scenery, I will write another time.

The collection consists of :—

  1. 1. திருமுருகாற்றுப்படை—Tirumurugāttupadai.
  2. 2. பொருக்காற்றுப்படை—Porunarāttupadai.
  3. 3. சிறு பாணாற்றுப்படை—Pānāttupadai (Minor).
  4. 4. பெரும்பாணாற்றுப்படை—Pānāttupadai (Major).
  5. 5. முல்லைப்பாட்டு—Mullai-pāttu.
  6. 6. மதுரைக்காஞ்சி—Madurai-Kānji.
  7. 7. நெபிநல்வாடை—Nedunal-Vādai.
  8. 8. குறிஞ்சிப்பாட்டு—Kuriñji-pāttu.
  9. 9. பட்டினப்பாலை—Paṭtina-pālai.

and 10. மலைபடுகடாம்—Malaipadu-kadām.

Of these the 1st is the work of the well known Nakkīrar (நக்கீரர்). In it he speaks of the greatness of Muruga and his own experiences as a devotee. It is in the form of instructions given to a pilgrim going in search of mōksha or heavenly bliss.
