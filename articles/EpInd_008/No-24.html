CHENDALUR PLATES OF SARVALOKASRAYA ; A.D. 673.

BY PROFESSOR E. HULTZSCH, PH.D.; HALLE (SAALE).

Like the Chendalûr plates of Kumáravishnu II. (No. 23 above), these copper-plates “were found buried in a pot full of paddy husk when a ryot of Chendalûr in the Ongole tâluka of the Nellore district was digging his house-site for laying foundation. This was some forty years ago.” The plates passed from the ryot to the Karnam of the village, and from the latter to Mr. N.
