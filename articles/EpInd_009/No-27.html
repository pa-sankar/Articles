BUCHKALA INSCRIPTION OF NAGABHATTA;
SAMVAT 872.

BY D. R. BHANDARKAR, M.A.; POONA.

This inscription was first discovered by a Brahmabhatta of Jôdhpur named Nannurâma whose zeal for antiquarian matters is as unfagging as it is disinterested. It was found at Buchkalâ in the Bilâdâ district, Jôdhpur State. It is incised on a pilaster on the proper right forming part of the shrine wall jutting out into the sabhâmandapa of what is popularly known there as the temple of Pârvatî.
