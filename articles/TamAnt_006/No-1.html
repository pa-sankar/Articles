INTRODUCTION.

THE extracts from Puřa-poruḷ Veņbā-Mālai and Puřa-nānūřu was originally published in the 'Asiatic Quarterly Review' and the 'Indian Antiquary' respectively and were sent to me by the late Rev. Dr. G. U. Pope, for publication in the 'Light of Truth or the Siddhānta Dīpikā,' and most of them were accordingly printed in the earlier volumes of this Magazine. They are now reprinted in book-form so as to be available to all students of Tamiḻ. Dr. Pope's contributions to Tamiḻ literature have been various and immense, and published in a number of Magazines, besides his large editions of 'Kuřaḷ' and 'Nālaḍiyār', 'Tiruvāçakam' and Tamiḻ Text-books and Tamiḻ Grammar, and in his last days he had prepared a translation of Paḷamoḷi about which he wrote to me and which he valued highly. He had revised his Kuřaḷ and Nālaḍiyār also. Dr. Pope's son Mr. John V. Pope, who is now in India, has kindly promised to send me all the collected papers and it is my intention to publish them from time to time; and it is hoped that this present brochure will serve to whet the appetite of the Tamiḻ public for the more that is to come.

I was in correspondence with the late Dr. Pope about bringing out an adequate account of his life and works and he wrote to me to say that he left this duty to be performed by his sons, and Mr. J. V. Pope has kindly undertaken this pious duty and has promised to send me instalments of the 'life' from June next and this I hope to publish in the Siddhānta Dīpikā.

Dr. Pope was a genuine scholar and worked hard till the last days of his long life. Being possessed of a generous heart he helped every one who came to him for help. The consequence was, he lived poor and died poor.
