Tēgūr (l. 49). On the name and site of Sogal see above. For the Kūṇḍi Three-thousand see I. A., Vol. XXIX, p. 278. Belavāḍi may be “Belwadi” (“Belowuddee” of the old Indian Atlas), which lies in Sāmpgāon tāluka, some 9½ miles SSW. from Sogal. Tēgūr may conceivably be one of two places of that name: there is a Tēgūr in Sāmpgāon tāluka, some 22 miles SW. from Sogal, and another in Dhārwār tāluka, about the same distance SSW. from Sogal; but both of them are too far to suit the requirements of our text. The other places cannot be identified.
