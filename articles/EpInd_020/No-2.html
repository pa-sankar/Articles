NALANDA STONE INSCRIPTION OF THE REIGN OF YASOVARMMADEVA.

By HIRANANDA SASTRI.

The inscription which forms the subject of this paper was excavated at Nālandā, the well-known ancient site of Magadha, by Mr. J. A. Page in the official year 1925-26.1 It was found buried in the debris of the southern verandah of the old vihāra—now called Monastery I—which has yielded not only a large number of bronze or copper images of various kinds and the very valuable copper-plate inscription of Dēvapāladēva that has been published above,2 but also the earliest remains so far discovered at Nālandā.
