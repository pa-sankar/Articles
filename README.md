# Articles

Public site: https://pa-sankar.github.io/Articles/

Epigraphical articles, read from the published volumes: Epigraphia Indica,
the Mysore Archaeological Reports and Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum.
Companion to [Inscriptions](https://pa-sankar.github.io/Inscriptions/).

The pages here are generated by the HeroSearch project's build scripts and
committed by them; edits made here by hand are overwritten.

Images are web-sized copies (the full scans stay with the project). The build
reports their total size and warns past 600 MB of the ~1 GB GitHub Pages budget.
