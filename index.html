Articles

The twin of Project InscriptionSearch

The scholarly articles in which inscriptions were first edited, read from the published volumes: the full text, searchable, with the plates and figures in place and every footnote kept.

Epigraphia Indica

The Archaeological Survey of India's journal of epigraphy: each article edits one or more inscriptions, with an introduction, the text, a translation and the plates. Now: 634 articles.

Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum

The Archaeological Survey of India's corpus series: Aśoka, the Kharoshthi and Bhārhut inscriptions, the Guptas, the Kalachuri-Chedi era and more, each volume with its editor's introduction and every inscription edited in full. Now: 162 articles.

Mysore Archaeological Reports

The yearly reports of the Mysore Archaeological Department: monuments, numismatics, manuscripts and the copper-plate grants discussed each year. Now: 17 reports.

Tamilian Antiquary

The Tamilian Archaeological Society's journal, 1907-1914: essays on Tamil history, literature, coins and the dates of the poets and kings. Now: 49 articles.
Please verify before you quote. These articles were read from the scanned volumes by machine (OCR) and put back together page by page. Mistakes may remain, most of all in Devanagari and other Indic script, and in the small footnote numbers. Please check the printed volume before using the text.
