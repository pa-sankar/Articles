Corpus Inscriptionum Indicarum is the Archaeological Survey of India's corpus series, from 1877: each volume gathers the inscriptions of one dynasty or era (Aśoka, the Kharoshthi records, the Guptas, the Kalachuri-Chedi era and others). Each numbered inscription is one article here, and each chapter of the editor's introduction is one article too.

Please verify before you quote. These articles were read from the scanned volumes by machine (OCR) and put back together page by page. Mistakes may remain, most of all in Devanagari and other Indic script, and in the small footnote numbers. Please check the printed volume before using the text.
